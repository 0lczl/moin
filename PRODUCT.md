# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Listeners of Arabic religious teaching who want to understand the meaning in English or French, and an operator who supplies a recording or chooses a Haramain stream.

## Product Purpose

Moin helps people follow Arabic speech through a visible Arabic transcript, meaning-focused English and French translations, and spoken output when the text is safe to play. The current web preview shows the intended recording and live-listening experience; it is a visual concept, not evidence that live YouTube translation is implemented.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and attentive. The interface should feel appropriate for careful listening and review.

## Anti-references

Avoid the retired mosque-silhouette experiments, generic AI dashboard styling, and visual claims of translation accuracy unsupported by the benchmark. The official presentation identity and `DESIGN-GUIDE.md` are the visual references.

## Design Principles

1. Put the listening task first.
2. Keep Arabic and translated text easy to compare.
3. Make uncertainty and illustrative content explicit.
4. Give Arabic and English equally complete layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Arabic right-to-left and English left-to-right layouts, readable contrast, keyboard focus, touch controls, and reduced motion. Keep Arabic text in its natural direction inside either language of interface.
